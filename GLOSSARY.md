# RenderBank

RenderBank is a visual-first library of curated AI image prompts and prompt packs for creating tested visual outcomes.

## Language

**Prompt**:
A curated, tested recipe for producing a specific AI-generated visual, including its text, variables, recommended model, settings, and example output.
_Avoid_: Template, text prompt, asset

**Free Prompt**:
A Prompt whose complete recipe is available to every Visitor without an account or purchase.
_Avoid_: Sample prompt, trial prompt

**Premium Prompt**:
A Prompt whose complete recipe is available through purchase of its Prompt Pack.
_Avoid_: Paid prompt, locked prompt

**Prompt Pack**:
A curated, one-time-purchase collection of related Premium Prompts built around a practical visual outcome or use case.
_Avoid_: Product, bundle, subscription

**Product Ads**:
The RenderBank content focus for commercial product imagery, including advertising, e-commerce, campaign, and product-photography outcomes.
_Avoid_: General product content

**Social Creative**:
The RenderBank content focus for Instagram-first, platform-ready social media visuals used by content creators and marketers.
_Avoid_: Social post, social media prompt

**Product Photography**:
A Prompt category for styled commercial imagery in which a physical product is the primary subject.
_Avoid_: Product, product content

**Ad Creative**:
A Prompt category for campaign and promotional visuals designed to communicate or sell an offer.
_Avoid_: Advertising, advertisement

**E-commerce Listing**:
A Prompt category for clear product visuals intended for marketplace and online-store listings.
_Avoid_: E-commerce, marketplace image

**Primary Category**:
The single category that best represents a Prompt's main intended visual outcome; secondary contexts are expressed as tags and use cases.
_Avoid_: Category list, multiple categories

**Visitor**:
A person browsing RenderBank without an account or purchase access.
_Avoid_: Guest, anonymous user

**Buyer**:
A person who purchases a Prompt Pack using an email address, without needing a RenderBank account.
_Avoid_: Customer, member, subscriber

**Buyer Library**:
The accountless view of the Prompt Pack and Premium Prompts unlocked by one purchase's secure access session.
_Avoid_: User library, account dashboard, collection

**Demo Content**:
Temporary prototype content that demonstrates a Prompt's intended structure and presentation but has not passed RenderBank's publishing quality gate.
_Avoid_: Tested prompt, published prompt
